Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-the-funniest-toaster-error-436406cd) bounty "The Funniest Toaster Error Message": one line, at most 120 characters, the error message a kitchen toaster shows its owner.

## Entry

> E14: Bread declined to brown and has requested a transfer to the freezer. No further comment.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output.
